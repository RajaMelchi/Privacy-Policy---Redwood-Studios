Official Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

1. Overview & Application Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games, mobile applications, and digital services developed and published by Redwood Studios ("we", "us", or "our"), including SumSprint and future titles.

We respect user privacy and are committed to protecting it. Our applications do NOT require account registration, login, or collection of personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, phone number, contacts, or physical location.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a seamless gaming experience, our applications save non-personal gameplay progress locally on your device (using native local storage or SharedPreferences). This includes:

  • Game level progress, star ratings, and high scores
  • Daily challenge streaks and unlocked achievements
  • User settings (audio, haptics, and custom visual themes)

This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our free-to-play applications integrate Google AdMob to display advertisements (such as banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads). AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal device information, including:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address for coarse geolocation and ad localization
  • Ad performance and interaction metrics

For more details on how Google handles advertisement data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases or Pro upgrades (such as removing advertisements) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. Redwood Studios does not collect, store, or have access to your payment card details or financial information.

5. Children's Privacy Compliance

Our applications are designed for general audiences and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
