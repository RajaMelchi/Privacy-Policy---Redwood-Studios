SumSprint

Official App Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

1. Overview & Data Collection

SumSprint ("we", "our") values user privacy. SumSprint does NOT require account registration, sign-in, or collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, phone number, physical address, or contacts.

All gameplay data—including level progression, star ratings, high scores, daily challenge streaks, sound preferences, and custom themes—is stored exclusively on your local device storage.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of SumSprint uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements (banners, interstitials, and rewarded video ads). AdMob may process non-personal device data, such as:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • Coarse location / IP address for ad localization
  • Ad interaction metrics

For more information on how Google manages ad data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional upgrades (such as SumSprint Pro for $1.99) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. SumSprint does not store, handle, or have access to your payment card details, banking information, or billing address.

4. Children's Privacy

SumSprint is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
